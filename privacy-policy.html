Privacy Policy

Goods Sort Puzzle · Published by Dari Studio · Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Goods Sort Puzzle (“the app”), published by Dari Studio (“we”), handles, and why. We built the app so that you can play without creating an account.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following data is saved locally on your device (in the app’s private storage) and is not sent to us:

  • Game progress: level, coins, stars, boosters, unlocked themes, colour palette and cosmetic items.
  • A randomly generated local player ID and the display name you choose (for example “Player_1234”).
  • Settings such as language, sound and reminder preferences.

The leaderboard is computed on your device. Your display name and scores are not uploaded to any server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of this information.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads, including optional rewarded videos, through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), app interactions, and diagnostic information. This data is processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

The app requests non-personalised ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Customize → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device (for example, when your daily gift is ready or a new weekly season starts). These reminders are created on the device; no push token is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app or in your system settings.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising data processed by Google AdMob described above, the app does not share data with third parties.

6. Security

Data handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Locally stored game data stays in the app’s private storage on your device.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete all local game data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Change ad consent under Customize → Privacy options (where applicable).
  • Opt out of personalised advertising and reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
