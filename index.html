Goods Sort Puzzle

Coming soon to Google Play

Tap items on the wooden shelves to move them to your tray. Collect three of a kind to clear them, and empty every shelf to finish the level.

The tray only holds seven items, so a little planning goes a long way.

  • Endless levelsEvery level is checked to be solvable.
  • Five colour palettesFrom Mystic Twilight to Obsidian Gold.
  • Weekly seasonsEarn shelf skins, tray borders and avatar frames.
  • No account neededOpen the game and play, even offline.

About the studio

Our games are available in English, French, German and Bulgarian.
